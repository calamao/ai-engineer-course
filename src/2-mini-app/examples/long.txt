A SaaS company is experiencing increased churn and support volume after rolling out a redesigned onboarding flow. The customer support queue is filled with tickets from users who cannot complete account setup because they are repeatedly asked to verify their email address even after a successful confirmation. The bug appears most often in browsers with extensions that block third-party cookies and in devices using a VPN. The product team believes the root cause is in the session validation logic, while the platform team suspects that the new security middleware is forcing a redirect loop after the verification callback. Engineering has already disabled the new route in staging, but the team is still reviewing whether an earlier change in the identity provider configuration is amplifying the issue. Because the problem affects a critical business milestone, leadership wants a quick mitigation, a precise diagnosis, and a permanent fix before the next release window.
